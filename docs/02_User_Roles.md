# User Roles and Responsibilities

This document defines the three primary user types in the Multi-Vendor E-Commerce Platform and outlines their specific capabilities and access levels.

## 1. Super Admin
The Super Admin has complete oversight and control over the entire platform.

**Capabilities:**
*   **Platform Management:** General settings and configurations.
*   **Vendor Approval:** Review, approve, or reject new vendor applications.
*   **Reports:** Access platform-wide sales, revenue, and analytics.
*   **Categories:** Manage global product categories and subcategories.
*   **Coupons:** Create and manage platform-wide discount coupons.

## 2. Vendor
Vendors are individual sellers operating their own stores within the platform.

**Capabilities:**
*   **Store Management:** Setup and manage their specific store profile and settings.
*   **Product Management:** Add, edit, delete, and organize their products.
*   **Inventory:** Update stock levels and manage product variants.
*   **Orders:** View and fulfill orders specifically placed for their products.
*   **Earnings:** Track revenue, payouts, and financial performance.

## 3. Customer
Customers are the end-users who browse the platform to purchase products.

**Capabilities:**
*   **Registration:** Create and manage a personal customer account.
*   **Shopping:** Browse products, search, and navigate categories across all vendors.
*   **Cart:** Add products to the shopping cart and manage quantities.
*   **Checkout:** Place orders securely and process payments.
*   **Reviews:** Leave ratings and reviews for purchased products.
